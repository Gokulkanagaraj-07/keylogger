# keylogger

  A keylogger, short for keystroke logger, is a type of surveillance technology used to monitor and record each keystroke typed on a keyboard. Keyloggers can be software-based or hardware-based, and they often operate in the background without the user’s knowledge. While they can be used for legitimate purposes, such as monitoring employee activity or troubleshooting technical issues, they are more commonly associated with malicious activities. Cybercriminals use keyloggers to capture sensitive information, like passwords and credit card numbers, leading to identity theft and financial fraud.

It's essential to have robust security measures in place, such as using antivirus software and keeping your system updated, to protect against keyloggers.
